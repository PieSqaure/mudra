How it works

One recognition loop, running entirely on the phone.

The heavy models sit on the Snapdragon Hexagon NPU; the rest is light work on the CPU. The same pipeline powers both the in-room and on-call paths.

01

Watch the hands

The camera feeds a hand-pose model that reads signs from a curated, high-value vocabulary.

NPU · MediaPipe
02

Confirm the sign

A classifier maps the motion to a phrase and shows it back — low confidence asks you to repeat.

CPU · classifier
03

Speak it aloud

Android's on-device TTS voices the phrase — to the room, or down a live call.

Android TTS
04

Caption the reply

Whisper or Moonshine transcribes the other person on-device, so you read what they said.

Whisper · offline
Everything inside the dashed boundary runs on the phone. Only the ordinary phone call uses the network — recognition, transcription, and speech never touch a server.
Two modes

Talk to the room. Or make the call.

Most tools stop at translating signs to text. MUDRA+ closes the loop — a real two-way conversation, whether the other person is in front of you or on the line.

● In the room

A conversation, without a hearing helper

Sign to a shopkeeper, a guard, a pharmacist. The phone speaks for you; their reply comes back as captions you read.

  • → Sign → spoken aloud
  • → Their voice → captions, on-device
confirm before dialling
● On the phone — the voice agent

The call was always closed to them. Now it isn't.

The user signs a message; MUDRA+ places the call and becomes their voice on the line — then captions the reply back.

  • → Emergency, delivery, pharmacy, transport
  • → A Deaf person completing a call alone
Call use cases

The phone can talk for you.

Call mode turns a sign into a completed phone call — the situations where being unable to phone someone hurts most.

CONFIRM FIRST
🚑

Emergency

“I need an ambulance at <address>”

After an explicit confirmation, the phone calls the service line, speaks it, and captions the dispatcher back.

📦

Delivery helper

“Leave it with the guard”

Calls the rider waiting at the gate, relays the instruction, and captions their reply.

💊

Pharmacy & clinic

“Confirm my appointment”

Calls the clinic or pharmacy, asks the question, and shows the answer on screen.

🛺

Transport

“Pickup at <landmark>”

Calls the driver, gives the location, and captions the ETA back to the user.

🏠

Family & services

“I'm reaching in ten minutes”

A short signed message becomes a spoken call to anyone in the user's contacts.

📄

A record of it all

Conversation log export

Every call becomes a shareable transcript — what was signed, what was said, when.

Privacy by architecture

We won't have your hands. We'll have your voice.

Recognition, transcription, and speech all execute on the phone. No hand video, no call audio, no conversation is ever uploaded — because none of it needs to be. Every model is open: MediaPipe, Whisper, Android TTS.
